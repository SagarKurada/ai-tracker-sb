The Thesis

AI infrastructure is largely priced in. The next wave of value creation is in the companies using AI to automate the boring, rule-based work inside the world's largest industries — Healthcare, Manufacturing, Legal, and Energy.
The portfolio focuses on companies at the intersection of large total addressable market, low current AI adoption, and limited stock re-rating relative to the opportunity ahead.


Two Strategies
Equal Weight — equal capital allocated to each position, simple and unbiased. If the thesis is right across the board, this captures it cleanly.
Score-Weighted — allocation proportional to SummitBay Capital's proprietary Opportunity Score. Higher conviction = larger position.
The gap between the two strategies over time answers a real question: does sizing by conviction add value over simply spreading equally?

The Opportunity Score
The Opportunity Score is a quantitative framework developed by SummitBay Capital as part of its AI investment research process. It evaluates each company across three dimensions — industry TAM size, current AI adoption gap, and disruption potential — adjusted for how much the stock has already re-rated on AI expectations.
Score = (0.45 × TAM Score) + (0.35 × AI Adoption Gap Score) + (0.20 × Disruption Potential Score) − Rerating Penalty
Companies with the highest scores represent the largest unpriced AI opportunities. The score-weighted portfolio bets proportionally on that ranking being correct.
